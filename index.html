Semi Truck Loading Calculator

Plan tube shipments so every crate is forklift-safe on site and the trailer never overloads or over-stacks.

Truck & Site Constraints

Whichever stacking limit is most restrictive — max stack height, max crates per stack, or how high the forklift's forks can reach to get under the top crate — is applied. DOT axle-weight distribution isn't calculated here — even under total payload, keep heavier crates centered over the trailer's axles and spread weight front-to-back, not all at the nose or tail.

Tube Groups

Add one group per tube size/material. Each group is packed into its own crates, then all crates are loaded onto trucks together.

Generic / Miscellaneous Crates

Anything else riding on the same truck that isn't a tube bundle — tools, spare parts, hardware, blocking. Enter its weight directly; it's checked against the forklift and folded into the same loading plan.

Loading Plan

All figures are estimates for planning purposes — verify against actual crate builds, scale weights, and DOT/carrier requirements before shipping.
